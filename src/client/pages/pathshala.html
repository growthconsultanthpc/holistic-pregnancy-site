Har Ghar Mein Gurukul

Because a child’s first learning begins long before birth

In the journey of pregnancy, parents are a baby’s first guides. The thoughts you nurture, the emotions you hold, the values you live and the environment you create all become part of the world your baby experiences.

Holistic Pathshala is designed to help parents-to-be become more conscious, aware and prepared for this role.

Think of it as a modern Gurukul for parents, helping you nurture the right mindset, habits, values and emotional environment for the little shishya growing within.

You are the first gurus.Your home is the first gurukul.And the journey begins even before birth.

Why it matters

Your baby feels what you feel

Everyone today lives with some stress, and expecting mothers are no exception. Research increasingly links high stress and anxiety in pregnancy with changes in the developing baby.

That is why Holistic Pathshala puts the mother’s peace of mind at the centre: helping couples stay happy, positive and peaceful through the whole pregnancy, while they learn, prepare and grow together.

A calmer, more supported mother creates a more nurturing environment for the growing child. Holistic Pathshala gives couples the tools to build that calm, week after week.

What the program covers

Six areas, woven together across 21 weekly sessions, for the mind, body and soul of the mother, the child and the family

01Calm mind
  • Understanding stress and how it affects you and your baby
  • Meditation, breathing and relaxation you can use every day
  • Mindfulness and positive thinking
  • Handling worries, mood changes and fears with more ease
02Know your pregnancy
  • How your baby grows, stage by stage
  • The changes in your body and how to care for them
  • What to expect in each trimester
  • Making the most of your doctor’s guidance
03Nourished body
  • Nutrition for you and your growing baby
  • A healthy daily routine (dinacharya)
  • Gentle movement and physical care, as your doctor advises
  • Rest, sleep and energy
04Garbh Sanskar & bonding
  • Garbh Sanskar wisdom, explained simply and inclusively
  • Talking, reading and singing to your baby
  • Music, visualisation and conscious connection
  • Building the mother-baby bond from early pregnancy
05Together as a couple
  • The father’s role, every week of the journey
  • Communication and support between partners
  • Shared practices you do together
  • Preparing your home and family for the baby
06Life skills for parenthood
  • Patience, emotional balance and gratitude
  • Values and intentions for your child
  • Preparing mentally and emotionally for birth
  • Confidence for the life ahead as parents

The program complements your doctor’s care and never replaces it.

Learn. Experience. Practise.

Holistic Pathshala is not a lecture series. Every week combines three things.

Learn

Clear, practical knowledge about your pregnancy, your baby and yourselves, shared live in every session.

Experience

Guided meditations, visualisations, bonding activities and couple experiences that you feel, not just hear about.

Practise

Simple practices to carry into the week, so calm and connection become part of everyday life.
