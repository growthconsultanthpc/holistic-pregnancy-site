Meet the founder

Geetha Bafna

  • Certified Prenatal Yoga Trainer (RPYT)
  • Pregnancy & Garbh Sanskar Coach
  • Educationalist
  • 1,000+ mothers mentored

Geetha Bafna is a Montessori educator, conscious parenting guide and mother of two. With a background in commerce and training in Indian Montessori methods, she founded and leads a Montessori House and preschool focused on holistic development.

She has mentored more than 1,000 mothers through her Garbh Sanskar and Holistic Pregnancy programs, helping them build strong emotional and spiritual bonds with their unborn children.

Blending traditional Indian wisdom with modern science, she promotes mindful pregnancy and conscious parenting as the foundation for raising emotionally intelligent, value-driven children and building connected, compassionate families.

Our story
