Why prepare early

Give yourselves time to prepare

The Holistic Pregnancy Pre-Conception Program is designed for couples who want to prepare themselves before conception, not only physically, but emotionally and spiritually too. Because preparing for a baby can begin much before the positive test.

The founder’s philosophy is simple: the more consciously a couple prepares before conception, the stronger the foundation they can create for the journey ahead.

6months

Ideally, preparation begins at least six months before conception, and where possible, even earlier.

This is not about creating pressure around conception. It is about giving yourselves the time to build healthier habits, understand your bodies better, reduce unnecessary stress and step into parenthood with greater awareness.

A holistic approach to becoming parents

Preparation across three interconnected areas

Body

Build healthier everyday habits around food, movement, exercise and overall physical wellbeing.

Mind

Understand the role of stress, develop practices that support calmness, and learn meditation and visualisation techniques for greater emotional readiness.

Soul

Explore conscious conception and the deeper emotional and spiritual intention behind welcoming a new life.

What the program covers

Across 6 guided sessions, couples are taken through the key foundations of pre-conception preparation

  • Healthy eating habits and everyday nutrition awareness
  • The importance of regular movement and exercise
  • Working alongside your doctor to understand any existing health concerns and support your overall health and wellbeing
  • Stress awareness and guided meditation
  • Visualisation as a tool for intention, calmness and emotional preparation
  • Conscious conception
  • Preparing together as a couple
  • Bringing body, mind and soul into greater alignment before the pregnancy journey

One dedicated session

Conscious conception

This session encourages couples to look beyond the physical act of conception and reflect on their readiness to welcome and nurture a new life.

The focus is on intention, connection, emotional preparation and creating a supportive environment for the journey ahead.

For both of you

Fathers-to-be are part of this program

Pre-conception preparation is not only a woman’s responsibility. A father’s health, habits, stress levels and emotional readiness shape the start of the journey just as much, so fathers-to-be are also advised to participate in the program alongside their partner.

Both partners are encouraged to participate, learn together and understand how their individual wellbeing, habits, emotions and relationship shape the environment in which they begin their journey towards parenthood.

Who is this for?

This program may be right for you if

  • You are thinking about starting a family in the coming months
  • You want to prepare before conception, rather than begin only after pregnancy
  • You want the father-to-be to be equally involved in the journey
  • You want support that considers physical, emotional and spiritual wellbeing together
  • You are looking for a more conscious and intentional approach to parenthood

What this program does, and does not, promise

The Pre-Conception Program does not promise conception, fertility outcomes or a particular pregnancy result. It is designed to help couples feel better prepared across body, mind and emotional wellbeing as they move towards parenthood.

It complements appropriate medical care and is not a replacement for doctors, fertility specialists or other qualified healthcare professionals.

Your pregnancy journey does not have to begin with a positive test. It can begin with preparation.

With healthier habits.  With calmer minds.  With stronger connection.  With intention.
