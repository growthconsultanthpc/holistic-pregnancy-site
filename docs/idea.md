# Idea — Holistic Pregnancy Course website

**What it is.** A fast, static marketing and lead-generation site for *Holistic Pregnancy* (`holisticpregnancycourse.com`), a maternal-wellness programme led by founder Geetha Bafna. It guides couples from preconception through pregnancy (and early postpartum) with live online programmes and a WhatsApp community. It replaces the client's WordPress site.

**Who it's for.** Couples and women planning a pregnancy, currently pregnant, or early postpartum. Mostly India, mostly on a phone, arriving from Instagram and WhatsApp. Partners are explicitly welcome.

**Goal of the site.** Build trust, explain four stage-based programmes, and convert visitors into leads: free-session registrations and call requests. Enrolment is completed offline / via WhatsApp (see PRD decision D2 on the manual-payment checkout in the design).

**Success looks like.**
- Visitor picks the right programme in under a minute (stage chooser on the home page).
- Every lead lands in the CRM with its source (UTM) and stage; none are lost.
- Client edits copy, prices, FAQs, session dates in Sanity without a developer.
- Lighthouse mobile 90+; clean, indexable URLs.

**Also:** the first instance of a reusable template for wellness/course sites, so brand and copy live in tokens + CMS, never in components.
