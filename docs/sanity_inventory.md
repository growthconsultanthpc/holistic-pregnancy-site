# What you can edit on your website

Your website's content lives in **Sanity**, an editing screen where you change text, images, prices and settings and press **Publish**. The site updates by itself a few minutes later. No developer needed.

The design and layout are fixed, so nothing you type can break the look. Each field has a length guide and warns you if text is too long.

## Settings you control
| Area | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Business details** | Name, tagline, logo, phone, email, WhatsApp number, social links (Instagram, Facebook, Threads), footer text |
| **Book a Free Call** | Your Calendly link. Every "Book a Free Call" button uses it |
| **Announcement bar** | Switch on or off, change the text and link |
| **Next free session** | Choose **No date** (the site shows "date sent on WhatsApp") or **Show date** (pick date, time and an optional label). The date shows in the announcement bar, on the home page, on the free-session page, on the thank-you page and in the confirmation email. Once the date passes, the site switches back to "no date" automatically |
| **Payment instructions** | The text shown after enrolment and in the confirmation email (payment is collected offline) |

## Pages and content
| Page | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Headline and intro, button labels, the four trust points, the four "How it works" steps, founder text and photo, free-session text, which FAQs show |
| **Programmes (all four)** | Name, price, number and length of sessions, images, who it's for, the questions list, what it covers, **session plan (session number, title, short description, optional length)**, what you gain, the bullets in the price box, FAQs |
| **About, Community, Get in touch** | All text blocks. The Community page's WhatsApp link |
| **Forms** | Thank-you messages and the **three confirmation emails** (free session, enrol, contact): subject and body |
| **FAQs** | Question, answer, and which pages show it |
| **Testimonials** | Quote, name, stage, optional video link. The testimonials section appears only once at least **three** are published |
| **Legal pages** | Privacy Policy, Terms, Refund Policy, Medical Disclaimer: text, last-updated date, and a **Draft / Approved** switch. The site cannot go live while any is still Draft |
| **Blog** | Posts, categories, authors, images. A master switch **Show blog on site** keeps the blog completely hidden until you turn it on |
| **SEO (every page and post)** | Page title, short description, sharing image |

## Good to know about the editing screen
- Programmes and legal pages already exist; you edit them but can't delete or add new ones. The address of each page (its slug) is locked once set.
- Long text shows a yellow warning, it doesn't block you, but long text can break the page layout.
- Every image needs a short description (alt text).
- Videos are never uploaded here. Paste a YouTube link instead.

## Fixed by the developer (a change request)
Page layout, adding or removing sections, section order, brand colours, fonts, spacing, icons. Also the **Stage options** on forms (Planning / Pregnant / Not sure), because they must match your lead table.

## Your lead table (Airtable)
Every form submission lands there with its **source** and the programme chosen. Your team updates **Status** and **Payment status** by hand, adds call notes and follow-up dates, and can add leads that came from outside the website (events, Instagram, referrals). The free plan holds about 1,000 records; with your expected volume we recommend planning an upgrade around **month 4–5**.

## Good to know
- Edits go live a few minutes after Publish.
- Test emails and drafts can be previewed on the staging site first.
- Never type the Airtable or email passwords into Sanity; those stay private.
