WHY IT MATTERS

Pregnancy in a stress-filled world

What the numbers reveal about expecting mothers today

27%
experience depressive symptoms
37%
experience anxiety symptoms
26%
experience stress in at least one trimester
91%
remain unaware of perinatal depression

Global figures for pregnant women. Sources: The Guardian; National Library of Medicine.

Fear that never fades

Constant worry about every symptom and every scan can overshadow the joy of pregnancy.

Emotional overwhelm

Hormones fluctuate and bodies transform, and many mothers struggle silently.

Pressure to get everything right

Eat right, exercise, avoid stress: one misstep can bring guilt instead of support.

Feeling alone, even when surrounded

“Be happy you’re pregnant” leaves little room to talk about real fears.

A silent impact on the baby

A mother’s stress reaches her growing child. Emotional wellbeing is part of a healthy pregnancy.

These are not just emotional concerns. They affect the health of both mother and baby, and most antenatal care does not address them. That is where Holistic Pregnancy comes in.

GARBH SANSKAR

Nurturing life before birth

An ancient Indian practice of prenatal care, supported by what modern research tells us about life in the womb

What we know
Babies begin to hear sounds from around 18–20 weeks
They recognise their mother’s voice and respond to her emotions
Maternal stress hormones cross the placenta and affect development
Positive emotions release endorphins that benefit mother and baby
A mother’s nutrition directly shapes the baby’s brain growth

The goal: a healthy mother, a healthy baby and a strong bond.

Womb: the first school of life

A child’s Intelligence Quotient (IQ), Emotional Quotient (EQ) and Spiritual Quotient (SQ) begin forming in the womb, the foundation of a human being.

Shaped by every feeling you hold

A child in the womb absorbs the mother’s thoughts, emotions and environment. That is why prenatal education matters.

How does the course help when planning a pregnancy, and during pregnancy?

Planning a pregnancy: our Pre-conception Program helps you and your partner prepare physically, mentally and emotionally before you conceive, with nutrition, everyday movement, stress awareness, meditation and conscious conception, working alongside your doctor.

During pregnancy: our programs help you stay calm, positive and connected to your baby, understand each stage of your pregnancy, build healthy daily routines and feel more confident and prepared for labour, birth and parenthood.
