Why it matters

91%remain unaware of perinatal depression

Global figures for pregnant women. Sources: The Guardian; National Library of Medicine.

Fear that never fades

Constant worry about every symptom and every scan can overshadow the joy of pregnancy.

Emotional overwhelm

Hormones fluctuate and bodies transform, and many mothers struggle silently.

Pressure to get everything right

Eat right, exercise, avoid stress: one misstep can bring guilt instead of support.

Feeling alone, even when surrounded

“Be happy you’re pregnant” leaves little room to talk about real fears.

A silent impact on the baby

A mother’s stress reaches her growing child. Emotional wellbeing is part of a healthy pregnancy.

These are not just emotional concerns. They affect the health of both mother and baby, and most antenatal care does not address them. That is where Holistic Pregnancy comes in.

Garbh Sanskar

Nurturing life before birth

An ancient Indian practice of prenatal care, supported by what modern research tells us about life in the womb

What we know

  • Babies begin to hear sounds from around 18–20 weeks
  • They recognise their mother’s voice and respond to her emotions
  • Maternal stress hormones cross the placenta and affect development
  • Positive emotions release endorphins that benefit mother and baby
  • A mother’s nutrition directly shapes the baby’s brain growth

The goal: a healthy mother, a healthy baby and a strong bond.

Womb: the first school of life

A child’s Intelligence Quotient (IQ), Emotional Quotient (EQ) and Spiritual Quotient (SQ) begin forming in the womb, the foundation of a human being.

Shaped by every feeling you hold

A child in the womb absorbs the mother’s thoughts, emotions and environment. That is why prenatal education matters.

Questions, answered simply

How does the course help when planning a pregnancy, and during pregnancy?

Planning a pregnancy: our Pre-conception Program helps you and your partner prepare physically, mentally and emotionally before you conceive, with nutrition, everyday movement, stress awareness, meditation and conscious conception, working alongside your doctor.

During pregnancy: our programs help you stay calm, positive and connected to your baby, understand each stage of your pregnancy, build healthy daily routines and feel more confident and prepared for labour, birth and parenthood.

What do Holistic Pregnancy courses include?

Live online sessions led by Geetha Bafna and the team, in small batches of 5–6 mothers, covering pregnancy awareness, nutrition and daily routine (dinacharya), guided meditation and relaxation, prenatal music, gentle yoga, positive affirmations, Garbh Sanskar practices, bonding with your baby, couple practices and life skills for parenthood. Each program combines learning, guided experiences and simple practices to follow at home, with support from our team on WhatsApp.

Why choose Holistic Pregnancy? How are you different from other pregnancy courses?

Live, not recorded. Every session is held live by Geetha Bafna and our team, in small batches of 5–6 mothers, so you get personal attention and can ask your questions as you go.

Made for couples. Partners are included and encouraged to join, because the father’s health, habits and emotional readiness matter too.

Truly holistic. We care for body, mind and soul together: nutrition, daily routine (dinacharya), gentle yoga, meditation, prenatal music, affirmations and Garbh Sanskar, not just one topic on its own.

A program for every stage. From pre-conception to pregnancy, with post-partum support coming soon, so you can continue with people you already know and trust.

Experience you can trust. Led by founder Geetha Bafna, a Certified Prenatal Yoga Trainer (RPYT), educationalist and Garbh Sanskar coach, with 15+ years of experience and 1,000+ couples’ lives touched.

Honest and safe. Our programs work alongside your doctor’s care and never promise medical outcomes.

Do I have to start the course at a certain time? How long do I have to complete it?

Our programs run in live batches, so you join on a batch start date that our team confirms with you. The ideal time depends on your stage: Pre-conception is best started at least six months before you plan to conceive, Holistic Pathshala in early pregnancy, and 21 Days of Dinacharya or Power of 11 in any trimester. Each program runs for its set length: 6 guided sessions plus 1 free Conscious Conception session for Pre-conception, 21 days for Dinacharya, 11 weekly sessions for Power of 11 and 21 weekly sessions for Holistic Pathshala.

Yes. You can join one of our free live sessions to experience our approach before you enroll, or book a free call with our team to ask questions and find the program that fits you best.

Who can do the course?

Couples and individuals who are planning a pregnancy, are pregnant (in any trimester), or have recently had a baby. Partners are warmly encouraged to join, as many sessions are designed for both parents. Our programs work alongside your doctor’s care and do not replace medical advice.

No. Our programs work alongside the care of your doctor and are not a substitute for medical advice.
