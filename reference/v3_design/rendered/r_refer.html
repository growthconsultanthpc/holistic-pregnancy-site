Referral questions

Who can refer?

Anyone. You do not need to be in a program to refer a friend.

I have not joined a program. What do I get?

When your friend enrolls, you get a complimentary meditation session with us. Our team will reach out on WhatsApp to schedule it at a time that suits you.

When do I get my reward?

Once your friend’s enrolment and payment are confirmed, our team applies ₹1,000 off your course, or schedules your complimentary meditation session, and lets you know on WhatsApp.

Which programs count?

All of them. Your friend can join any of our programs: Preconception, Holistic Pathshala, 21 Days of Dinacharya or Power of 11.

How does my friend use my code?

If they open your link, the code is added automatically. Otherwise they can type it in the “Referral code” box at checkout, or mention it when our team calls.
