Har Ghar Mein Gurukul

Because a child’s first learning begins long before birth

In the journey of pregnancy, parents are a baby’s first guides. The thoughts you nurture, the emotions you hold, the values you live and the environment you create all become part of the world your baby experiences.

Holistic Pathshala is designed to help parents-to-be become more conscious, aware and prepared for this role.

Think of it as a modern Gurukul for parents, helping you nurture the right mindset, habits, values and emotional environment for the little shishya growing within.

You are the first gurus.Your home is the first gurukul.And the journey begins even before birth.

Why it matters

Your baby feels what you feel

Everyone today lives with some stress, and expecting mothers are no exception. Research increasingly links high stress and anxiety in pregnancy with changes in the developing baby.

That is why Holistic Pathshala puts the mother’s peace of mind at the centre: helping couples stay happy, positive and peaceful through the whole pregnancy, while they learn, prepare and grow together.

Questions, answered simply

When should we join?

Holistic Pathshala is designed for couples in early pregnancy, so the 21 weekly sessions can support the complete journey.

Is it suitable for a high-risk pregnancy?

Please speak with your doctor first. Our program supports, but never replaces, medical care.

Can my partner join?

Yes, and we encourage it. The father’s role, couple practices and family preparation are part of every stage of the program.

We are past early pregnancy. Can we still join?

Holistic Pathshala is designed to start in early pregnancy so you get the full 21 weeks. If you are further along, talk to our team and we will suggest the best fit, such as 21 Days of Dinacharya or Power of 11.

How are sessions held?

21 live sessions, one every week, 60 minutes each, held online as live virtual sessions.

Do I need to pay right away?

No. Tap Enroll Now and share your details. We will call you first, and you pay only after your questions are answered and your batch is confirmed.

Does this replace medical care?

No. The program complements care from your doctor and other qualified healthcare professionals.

What if I miss a session?

All our sessions are live, and recordings are not shared. We encourage you to attend every session live to get the full experience. If you know you will miss one, please let our team know in advance.

Holistic Pregnancy is educational support and works alongside, not instead of, care from your doctor.
