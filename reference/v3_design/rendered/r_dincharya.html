Our foundational program

Learn it. Practise it. Live it.

21 Days of Dinacharya is Holistic Pregnancy’s foundational program for couples in any trimester, designed to help you build simple, supportive pregnancy practices into your daily routine.

For 21 days, you are guided and monitored as you apply the learnings consistently, so they begin to become part of your Dinacharya, your everyday way of living through pregnancy.

21days

Why 21 days? When a new practice is followed consistently over a period of time, it becomes easier to integrate into daily life.

This course gives couples a structured 21-day framework to not only learn, but also practise and implement, with a focus on consistency, awareness and small daily actions.

What the program covers

Key themes couples are introduced to

  • Prenatal bonding
  • Prenatal memory
  • Stress management
  • Techniques to support and enhance energy levels
  • Healthy eating habits
  • Visualisation
  • Simple everyday practices to bring into your pregnancy routine

From learning to implementation

Not a one-time information session

The 21-day format helps couples stay engaged with the practices, reflect on what they are learning and build greater consistency through guided follow-through.

The intention is to make the learnings practical enough to become part of everyday pregnancy life.

Designed for any trimester

First, second or third trimester

Whether you are in your first, second or third trimester, 21 Days of Dinacharya can help you bring more structure, mindfulness and conscious habits into your pregnancy journey.

It is also a good starting point for couples who want to experience the Holistic Pregnancy approach in a simple, practical format.

For the mother. For the baby. For everyday pregnancy life.

Small practices, followed consistently

Through prenatal bonding, stress-management practices, healthy routines, visualisation and conscious daily habits, the program helps couples create a more supportive rhythm for the mother and baby.

The focus is on small practices, followed consistently, that can make the pregnancy journey feel more intentional and connected.

By the end of the 21 days, couples feel

  • Calmer, with simple practices to manage everyday stress
  • More energetic, with healthier daily routines
  • More connected to their baby through daily bonding
  • Confident and better prepared for labour and birth

Questions, answered simply

Which trimester is this for?

Any trimester. The program is designed for couples in the first, second or third trimester.

Can my partner join?

Yes, and we encourage it. Pregnancy is a shared journey, and fathers-to-be can make a big difference.

How does the 21-day support work?

You learn the practices and are then guided and monitored online as you apply them consistently over 21 days.

What comes after?

Couples who want to go deeper can continue with Power of 11.

Do I need to pay right away?

No. Tap Enroll Now and share your details. We will call you first, and you pay only after your questions are answered and your batch is confirmed.

Does this replace medical care?

No. The program complements care from your doctor and other qualified healthcare professionals.

What if I miss a session?

All our sessions are live, and recordings are not shared. We encourage you to attend every session live to get the full experience. If you know you will miss one, please let our team know in advance.

Holistic Pregnancy is educational support and works alongside, not instead of, care from your doctor.
