Supporting the mother

Nurturing the environment around the child

A mother’s pregnancy involves her body, thoughts, emotions, routines and relationships. Power of 11 brings these dimensions together rather than looking at pregnancy through a single lens.

The aim is to help the mother feel supported in mind, body and soul, while the couple consciously create a nurturing environment around their growing child.

Questions, answered simply

Do I need to complete 21 Days of Dinacharya first?

It is not required, but Power of 11 builds on the Dinacharya foundations, so many couples find it a natural next step.

Can my partner join?

Yes. Couples are encouraged to learn and participate together.

How are sessions held?

11 live guided sessions, one per week, 60 minutes each, held online as live virtual sessions.

Do I need to pay right away?

No. Tap Enroll Now and share your details. We will call you first, and you pay only after your questions are answered and your batch is confirmed.

Does this replace medical care?

No. The program complements care from your doctor and other qualified healthcare professionals.

What if I miss a session?

All our sessions are live, and recordings are not shared. We encourage you to attend every session live to get the full experience. If you know you will miss one, please let our team know in advance.

Holistic Pregnancy is educational support and works alongside, not instead of, care from your doctor.
