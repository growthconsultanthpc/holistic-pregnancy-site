BUILDS ON 21 DAYS OF DINACHARYA

More knowledge. More practice. Deeper connection.

Power of 11 is an 11-session holistic pregnancy program that gives expecting couples deeper knowledge, guided practices and practical tools to navigate pregnancy.

It takes the journey further, from creating healthy everyday practices to understanding and experiencing pregnancy more consciously, supporting the complete journey of the mother and child across mind, body and soul.

SUPPORTING THE MOTHER

Nurturing the environment around the child

A mother’s pregnancy involves her body, thoughts, emotions, routines and relationships. Power of 11 brings these dimensions together rather than looking at pregnancy through a single lens.

The aim is to help the mother feel supported in mind, body and soul, while the couple consciously create a nurturing environment around their growing child.

A JOURNEY FOR BOTH PARENTS

Partners are not spectators

Power of 11 encourages couples to learn and participate together, creating greater understanding, involvement and connection throughout pregnancy.

Because preparing for a child is also about preparing to become parents together.
